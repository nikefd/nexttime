# Milestone G — Today-First Personal Practice

Date: 2026-10-18

## Product decision

NextTime now focuses on individual practice. The primary experience is no longer a course catalog: after onboarding, the Today tab presents one locally selected practice and one clear start action.

## Experience changes

- Four tabs: Today, Progress, Learn, and Settings. The Practice catalog is removed from primary navigation.
- Today shows one recommended session, its duration, the reason for the recommendation, and a single start button.
- The six-week plan remains visible as compact progress instead of a large dashboard.
- The optional pre-practice check-in moves into an “Adjust today's intensity” sheet.
- Statistics and history remain in Progress rather than competing with the daily action on Today.
- New onboarding offers only three individual goals.
- New plans and maintenance recommendations use 18 individual sessions across foundation, relaxation, and confidence routes.

## Compatibility and safety

- Existing partner programs, records, and localization keys remain in the data model so previous local data can still be decoded.
- Partner routes and cards are not reachable from the primary product experience and are never selected by new plans.
- Educational content about communication, ongoing consent, boundaries, and safety remains available in Learn.
- Existing local-only storage, safety downgrade rules, application lock, and complete data deletion remain unchanged.
