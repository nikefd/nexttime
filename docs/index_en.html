Four paths

Control is not the whole story.

Contraction and relaxation receive equal attention. Start from your goals; check-ins are always optional.

01

Foundations

Identification, short contractions, endurance, and coordination.

02

Release & restore

Diaphragmatic breathing and letting go—more is not always better.

03

Confidence & awareness

Breathing and body scans that bring attention back to the present.

04

Practice together

Same-device cards about boundaries, consent, and appreciation.

Safety boundary

Wellness practice, not medical diagnosis.

NextTime does not diagnose, treat, or guarantee improvement of any condition. Stop and contact a qualified health professional if practice causes pain, bleeding, numbness, dizziness, or worsening symptoms. Contact local emergency services for urgent concerns.
