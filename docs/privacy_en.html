Privacy

Privacy Policy

Effective and last updated: October 12, 2026

In short: no account; practice, check-in, and partner-exercise records remain on your device; the developer does not receive them; no ads, third-party analytics, or cross-app tracking.

What we do not collect

NextTime does not require registration or login. The app does not send names, contact details, practice records, check-ins, goals, or partner-card content to developer servers. It does not use advertising SDKs, third-party behavioral analytics, HealthKit, location, camera, or microphone.

Data on your device

Practice history, plans, check-ins, and safety acknowledgement are stored in a local database protected by iOS Data Protection and excluded from device backups. Non-sensitive interface preferences use system preference storage. The app offers no CloudKit or developer-operated cloud sync.

Use Settings → Data → Clear All Data to erase records stored by the app. Removing the app normally removes its local container. Apple handles device and purchase records under its own policies.

System services and permissions

  • Notifications: used only after you enable reminders; discreet copy is the default.
  • Face ID / Touch ID: optional and evaluated by iOS; the app never receives biometric templates.
  • App Store: Apple processes purchases. The developer does not receive full payment-card details; Apple may provide purchase status to the app.

Website

This site is hosted on GitHub Pages and contains no advertising pixels, analytics scripts, or forms. GitHub may process essential server logs under the GitHub Privacy Statement.

Age and changes

The service is for adults aged 18 and over. If data practices change materially, we will update this page and its date and provide in-app notice when appropriate.

Contact

Email privacy questions to nikefd@gmail.com. Please do not include sexual history, medical history, or other sensitive health details.
