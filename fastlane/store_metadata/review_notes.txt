NextTime 1.5.0 is a wellness education and guided personal-practice app for adults. No account or demo credentials are required.

After the first-launch disclosures, open Today to start the current lesson or tap Browse all 24 lessons. The first stage has six free lessons. All lessons can be previewed. The existing one-time non-consumable Pro purchase unlocks free selection across all 24 lessons, reminders and detailed statistics. Select any lesson in the library to inspect its goal, sets and duration. The library also supports text search and on-device favorites.

The four-stage learning path advances after the user completes the current lesson and voluntarily confirms readiness. Other sessions are saved in history. In discreet practice, active audio can continue when the screen locks. Interactive checkpoints wait for the user.

Settings includes Restore Purchases, optional app lock and deletion of local training data. Training history and optional check-ins remain on-device. The app provides education and general wellness practice and does not diagnose or treat medical conditions.
